Privacy

Last updated: July 2026

Crash Cash is a simulation for learning about money. It is not a bank, it never touches real money, and it has no connection to any real financial account.

If you do not make an account

Nothing about you is collected or transmitted. Your run is stored in your own browser using local storage, which is a small amount of space on your own device. It never leaves that device. There are no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, and no third-party scripts on the page.

Clearing your browser data deletes your run. That is the only copy, so export a save file from Settings if you want to keep it.

If you do make an account

An account is entirely optional. It exists for one reason: so your run survives switching devices or clearing your browser. If you create one, two things are stored:

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in. If you sign in with Google instead, we receive your email address from Google and nothing else, no contacts, no profile, no browsing history.
  • Your game save: the fictional money in your simulation, the jobs you picked, and your settings. It is invented data about an invented character.

That is the complete list. No name is required, no address, no phone number, no real financial information. Do not enter real account numbers anywhere in this app; nothing here needs them.

Where it is stored

Accounts and saves are stored with Supabase, a hosted database service, on servers in the United States. Access is restricted at the database level so each account can read and write only its own save.

What is never done with it

  • It is never sold.
  • It is never shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • It is never used to market anything to you.
  • You will not be emailed except for the sign-in link you asked for.

Age

You must be 13 or older to create an account. This is a common line that keeps the project clear of the extra consent requirements that apply to younger children under the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act.

Everyone younger is still completely welcome to use Crash Cash. Every feature works without an account; the run simply stays on your own device, which is arguably the more private option anyway.

Deleting your data

In Settings, under Account, there is a Delete my account data button. It removes your cloud save immediately and signs you out. To remove the run on your device, use Start over in Settings or clear your browser data.

Contact

This is an open-source learning project. Questions, corrections, and deletion requests can be raised as an issue on the project's GitHub repository.

Nothing in Crash Cash is financial advice. It is a sandbox for building intuition safely.
